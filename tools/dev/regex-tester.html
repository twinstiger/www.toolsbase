g (global) finds all matches, not just the first. i makes it case-insensitive. m makes ^ and $ match line boundaries. s makes . match newlines too.
Parentheses () create capture groups. The first captured text appears in match[1], the second in match[2], etc. Use them to extract specific parts of your test string.
Check for escaping. In regex, special characters like \ . * + ? ^ $ { } [ ] | ( ) have special meaning. To match a literal dot, use \. not just . . Also check your flags — without the g flag, only the first match is returned.
