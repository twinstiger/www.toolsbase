Frequently Asked Questions

What is Base64 used for?+
Base64 encodes binary data as ASCII text — used for embedding images in HTML/CSS, transmitting data in JSON, email attachments, and API authentication headers.
Is my data sent to a server?+
No. All processing happens locally in your browser. Your data never leaves your device.
Does this support UTF-8?+
Yes. Full UTF-8 support including Chinese, emoji, and all Unicode characters.
