Frequently Asked Questions

How much can PDF size be reduced?+
Results depend on the original PDF. PDFs full of uncompressed high-res images can shrink 50-80%. Text-heavy PDFs typically shrink 5-20% from object stream optimization and metadata stripping. Already-optimized PDFs may grow slightly.
Does compression reduce quality?+
High mode is lossless — only the PDF structure is optimized. Medium and lower modes recompress embedded JPEG/PNG images, which slightly reduces image quality but is usually imperceptible. Vector text and shapes are never rasterized.
Why didn't my file get smaller?+
If the PDF was already heavily optimized by its creator (e.g., exported from modern word processors with "Save as PDF" using default settings), there may be little to gain. Try a lower quality setting — that often helps when structural optimization is exhausted.
Is my PDF safe?+
Yes. All processing happens locally in your browser using pdf-lib. Your file never leaves your device.
