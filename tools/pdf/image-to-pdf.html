Frequently Asked Questions

What image formats are supported?+
JPG, PNG, WebP, and GIF. Animated GIFs use the first frame. SVG is not supported (browsers don't expose SVG decoding for canvas use in this way).
How does "Image Fit" work?+
Contain fits the entire image inside the page with possible white space. Cover fills the page, cropping overflow. Stretch fills the page but distorts the image to match. Most of the time, "Contain" is what you want.
Can I reorder images?+
Yes. Drag any image by its thumbnail in the list to reorder. The PDF will be created in the displayed order, top-to-bottom, left-to-right.
Is my data safe?+
Yes. Images never leave your browser. All conversion happens locally using pdf-lib.
