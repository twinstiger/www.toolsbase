Frequently Asked Questions

How does PDF merge work?+
All processing happens locally in your browser using the pdf-lib library. We never upload your files to any server. The merged PDF is generated in memory and downloaded directly to your device.
Is there a limit on file size or number of files?+
No hard limits. Since everything runs in your browser, performance depends on your device's memory. Most modern devices can handle 10+ PDFs totaling several hundred MB.
Can I reorder files before merging?+
Yes. After uploading, simply drag any file card up or down to reorder them. The merge order matches the list order from top to bottom.
Is my data safe?+
Absolutely. Your files never leave your device. All processing is client-side, and the resulting PDF is generated and downloaded directly from your browser.
