Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between PNG and JPEG?+
PNG is lossless and best for documents, screenshots, and anything with text or sharp edges. JPEG is smaller and best for photos or scanned content. WebP offers the best size/quality tradeoff for the web.
What DPI should I choose?+
72 DPI is screen-resolution. 150 DPI is good for most everyday uses. 300 DPI is print quality and produces much larger files. Higher DPI = sharper image + bigger file.
How do I download all pages at once?+
Browsers don't allow multiple simultaneous downloads in a single click. Click "Download" on each image individually, or click "Download All" to trigger them in sequence (some browsers may prompt for permission).
Is my PDF safe?+
Yes. All processing happens in your browser using pdfjs-dist. Your file never leaves your device.
