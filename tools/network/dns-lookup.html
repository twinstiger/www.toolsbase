Frequently Asked Questions

What is DNS Lookup used for?+
DNS Lookup queries DNS records for any domain. Supports A, AAAA, CNAME, MX, TXT, NS record types and shows the full DNS resolution chain.
Is my data sent to a server?+
No. All processing happens locally in your browser. Your data never leaves your device — zero server communication.
Is this free to use?+
Yes. DNS Lookup is completely free, no sign-up required, no rate limits, no ads. Just open and use.
