❓ FAQ

What is my IP address?
Your IP (Internet Protocol) address is a unique number assigned to your device by your Internet Service Provider. It's used to identify and communicate with your device on the internet.
Is this my public or private IP?
This tool shows your public IP address — the one visible to external websites. Your private/local IP (like 192.168.x.x) is only visible within your home network.
Is my data stored?
No — your IP and location data are fetched in real-time from a geolocation API and never stored on our servers.
