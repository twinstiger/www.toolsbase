❓ FAQ

What does CIDR notation mean?
/24 means the first 24 bits are the network portion, leaving 8 bits for hosts. A /24 network has 256 addresses (254 usable). A /30 has 4 addresses (2 usable). The smaller the CIDR number, the larger the network.
Why do usable hosts = total - 2?
The first address (network address) and last address (broadcast address) are reserved. For example, in 192.168.1.0/24, .0 is the network and .255 is the broadcast — both can't be assigned to devices.
