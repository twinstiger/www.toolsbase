📱 QR Code Generator

Generate QR codes for URLs, text, WiFi credentials, or contact info. Download as PNG or SVG.

❓ FAQ

Is my data sent to a server?
No — QR codes are generated locally in your browser using JavaScript. Your data never leaves your device.
What formats can I download?
You can download as PNG (raster image) or SVG (vector format). SVG scales to any size without losing quality.
