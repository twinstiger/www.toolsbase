❓ FAQ

What file format should I use?
PNG is the most compatible modern format. ICO is best for Windows favicons. For most websites, a 32x32 or 48x48 PNG is sufficient.
Where do I upload the favicon?
Place your favicon file in the root folder of your website as favicon.ico. Most browsers automatically look for it there.
