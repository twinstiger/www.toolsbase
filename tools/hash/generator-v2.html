#️⃣ Hash Generator

Generate MD5, SHA-1, SHA-256, SHA-512, and more — instantly in your browser. No uploads.

🔐 Security Note

MD5 and SHA-1 are not recommended for passwords — they are fast and can be brute-forced. For passwords, use bcrypt or Argon2. SHA-256 and SHA-512 are fine for integrity checksums. All hashing is done locally in your browser — data never leaves your device.
