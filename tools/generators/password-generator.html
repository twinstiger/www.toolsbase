Frequently Asked Questions

Entropy measures password strength in bits. Each bit doubles the number of guesses needed to crack your password. A password with 80 bits of entropy would take a supercomputer billions of years to brute-force.
80 bits is the practical minimum for strong passwords. 100+ bits is better. A 16-character password with all character types provides ~100 bits. For critical accounts (banking, email), aim for 24+ characters.
This removes characters that are commonly confused with each other: uppercase I, lowercase L, number 1, uppercase O, and number 0. Useful when reading passwords aloud or on phone support calls.
