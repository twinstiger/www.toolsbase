Frequently Asked Questions

What is Lorem Ipsum used for in design?+
Lorem Ipsum is placeholder text that allows designers to focus on layout, typography, and visual hierarchy without getting distracted by content decisions. Since it is Latin text that readers cannot understand semantically, stakeholders can evaluate visual treatments without triggering emotional or editorial responses.
Where does Lorem Ipsum text come from?+
The classic Lorem Ipsum comes from Cicero's "de Finibus Bonorum et Malorum" (On the Ends of Goods and Evils), written in 45 BC. It became the industry standard through centuries of use in type specimen books and has been used in digital design since the early days of the web.
What input formats does this tool accept?+
The tool accepts natural language descriptions like "3 paragraphs", "100 words", "5 sentences", or "500 bytes". It will detect the format from your input and generate the appropriate amount of placeholder text. Default is 3 paragraphs if no format is specified.
Should I use fixed or random Lorem Ipsum?+
Randomized Lorem Ipsum provides varied word lengths and sentence structures that more closely mimics real prose, helping designers evaluate how text flows in layouts. Fixed passages provide consistency for comparing designs across sessions. Choose based on your workflow needs.
How much placeholder text should I use for realistic mockups?+
Use consistent placeholder lengths that reflect realistic use cases. If a content block is meant for 50 words, use approximately 50 words, not a full paragraph. For responsive designs, generate content quantities appropriate for each breakpoint. This helps identify spacing issues early in the design process.
Is my data sent to a server?+
No. All text generation happens locally in your browser using JavaScript. Your input and generated text never leave your device.
