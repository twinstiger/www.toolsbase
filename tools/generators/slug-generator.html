❓ FAQ

What is a URL slug?
A slug is the part of a URL that identifies a page in a human-readable way. For example, in /blog/my-blog-post, "my-blog-post" is the slug.
Does it handle non-English characters?
Yes — accented characters (é, ñ, ü) are converted to ASCII equivalents (e, n, u). Chinese/Japanese characters are romanized or removed.
