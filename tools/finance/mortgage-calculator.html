Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is this mortgage calculator?+
This calculator provides estimates based on principal and interest. Actual payments may include property taxes, insurance, and PMI. Check with your lender for exact figures.
What is a good down payment percentage?+
20% or more is ideal to avoid PMI (Private Mortgage Insurance). Some loans allow as low as 3-5% down, but you'll typically pay more in interest and insurance.
Is my data saved anywhere?+
No. All calculations happen locally in your browser. Your financial information never leaves your device.
