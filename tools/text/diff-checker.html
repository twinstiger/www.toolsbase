Frequently Asked Questions

What is Diff Checker used for?+
Diff Checker compares two texts and shows the differences line by line. Great for comparing code, documents, or any text content.
Is my data sent to a server?+
No. All processing happens locally in your browser. Your data never leaves your device — zero server communication.
Is this free to use?+
Yes. Diff Checker is completely free, no sign-up required, no rate limits, no ads. Just open and use.
