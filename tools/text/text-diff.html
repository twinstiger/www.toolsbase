Frequently Asked Questions

What is Text Diff used for?+
Text Diff highlights the exact differences between two text blocks — inserted text, deleted text, and unchanged portions are shown with distinct colors.
Is my data sent to a server?+
No. All processing happens locally in your browser. Your data never leaves your device — zero server communication.
Is this free to use?+
Yes. Text Diff is completely free, no sign-up required, no rate limits, no ads. Just open and use.
