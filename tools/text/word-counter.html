Frequently Asked Questions

What is Word Counter used for?+
Word Counter analyzes text to provide statistics like word count, character count, sentence count, and estimated reading time.
Is my data sent to a server?+
No. All processing happens locally in your browser. Your data never leaves your device.
Is this free to use?+
Yes. Word Counter is completely free, no sign-up required, no rate limits, no ads.
