❓ FAQ

What is bcrypt and why is it used?
bcrypt is a password hashing algorithm designed for security. It uses a work factor (cost) that makes hashing slow and resistant to brute-force attacks. It's the recommended algorithm for password storage in modern applications.
What cost factor should I use?
Cost 10 is the minimum recommended for new systems. Cost 12 is better for high-security applications. Higher costs are more secure but slower. Avoid cost below 10.
Is my password sent to a server?
No — bcrypt.js runs entirely in your browser. Your password never leaves your device.
