Frequently Asked Questions

What is Base32?+
Base32 is an encoding that uses 32 ASCII characters (A-Z, 2-7) to represent binary data as text. Often used in DNS, API keys, and TOTP secrets.
Is my data sent to a server?+
No. All processing happens locally in your browser.
