❓ FAQ

What's the difference between RGB and HSL?
RGB defines colors by Red, Green, Blue values (0-255 each). HSL defines by Hue (color angle), Saturation (vividness), and Lightness (brightness). HSL is often easier for humans to reason about.
What does alpha control?
Alpha controls opacity — 0% is fully transparent, 100% is fully opaque. RGBA and HSLA add an alpha channel to standard RGB/HSL.
