Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to create an account to use the tools?

No! All tools on Tools Base are completely free to use without registration. Just visit the site and start using them immediately.

Is my data safe when using your tools?

Absolutely! All processing happens locally in your browser. Your data never leaves your device or gets uploaded to any server. We take privacy very seriously.

Can I use these tools offline?

Once you've loaded a page, most tools will work offline since they run entirely in your browser. However, an internet connection is needed initially to load the page.

Are there any usage limits?

No! Use our tools as much as you want. There are no daily limits, no rate limiting, and no premium tiers. Everything is 100% free.

How can I support Tools Base?

The best way to support us is by sharing our tools with others who might find them useful. You can also provide feedback and suggestions to help us improve.
