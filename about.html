About ToolsBase

Free browser-based tools for developers, built with simplicity and privacy in mind.

Our Mission

ToolsBase gives developers a fast, reliable, and private set of browser-based tools. Every tool runs entirely client-side — your data never leaves your device. No accounts, no tracking, no uploads.

Privacy First

We don't use cookies, analytics, or tracking scripts. We don't know who uses our tools or what data is processed. Your privacy is not just a feature — it's the foundation of everything we build.

Fast by Design

All tools load instantly and run client-side. No server processing, no API roundtrips. What you paste stays on your machine. That's also what makes ToolsBase free to use forever.
