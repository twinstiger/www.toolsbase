Our Commitment to Privacy

Privacy is at the core of everything we do. Unlike many online tools that require you to upload your data to their servers, Tools Base processes all information directly in your web browser using client-side JavaScript.

This means:

  • Your sensitive data (passwords, API keys, personal information) never leaves your device
  • All calculations and transformations happen locally
  • No server-side processing or storage of your input data
  • You can use our tools offline once the page is loaded
