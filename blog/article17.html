Image Optimization for Web Performance

Development • May 2026

Images often constitute the majority of page weight. Learn to optimize them for lightning-fast websites.

Image Formats

  • JPEG - Photos, complex images
  • PNG - Transparency needed
  • WebP - Modern, superior compression
  • AVIF - Latest, best compression

Techniques

  1. Compress before upload
  2. Use responsive images (srcset)
  3. Lazy loading
  4. CDN delivery

Sizes
